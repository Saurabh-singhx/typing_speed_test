# KEYOPS // Tactical Typing Speed Test & APM Benchmark

> **Pro-Grade Tactical Typing Lab** — Zero Distracting Neon Glow, 100% Mechanical Precision.

KEYOPS is a high-performance typing speed test and keyboard benchmark engineered for competitive esports gamers, software developers, and mechanical keyboard enthusiasts. It combines the distraction-free responsiveness of Monkeytype with gamified features inspired by TypeRacer and Nitro Type, wrapped in a tactical military/mech HUD aesthetic.

---

## 🎯 Key Features & Competitor Enhancements

### 1. Zero-Latency Typing Engine (Inspired by Monkeytype)
- **Fluid Caret Dynamics**: Supports 4 distinct caret styles (`line`, `block`, `underline`, `box`) with smooth transitions.
- **Multiple Test Modes**:
  - **Time**: 15s, 30s, 60s, 120s
  - **Words**: 10, 25, 50, 100
  - **Quote**: Curated quotes from sci-fi lore and programming philosophy
  - **Boss Raid**: Gamified combat defense mode against rogue AI sentinels
- **Modifiers**: Toggle Punctuation, Numbers, and Sudden Death (Hardcore).
- **Hardcore Sudden Death**: A single typo aborts the mission—trains extreme precision under pressure.
- **Operator Shortcuts**: `Tab + Enter` or `Esc` to instantly restart, `Ctrl + Backspace` to wipe word.

### 2. Live Pacing Ghost Racer (Inspired by TypeRacer & Nitro Type)
- Real-time HUD race track showing your live avatar vs. a customizable **Target Ghost** (e.g., 60, 80, 100, 120 WPM) and your **Personal Best (PB)**.
- Live delta badge calculating exact cadence distance (`+8 WPM AHEAD` or `-3 WPM BEHIND`).

### 3. Procedural Mechanical Switch Sound Synthesizer (Web Audio API)
- Pure browser-synthesized acoustics with subtle pitch jitter—zero external MP3 assets, zero network latency:
  - **Linear Thock**: Deep, resonant low-frequency mechanical bottom-out.
  - **Clicky Blue**: Sharp high-frequency click with tactile snapping clack.
  - **Topre Dome**: Damped capacitive cushioned dome sound.
  - **Arcade 8-Bit**: Retro synthesizer chirp.
  - **Silent / Mute**: Quiet mode.
- Volume slider and rising chord fanfares on 10x, 25x, and 50x clean streaks.

### 4. Tactical Boss Raid Mode (Extra Unique Touch)
- Type words under time pressure to fire kinetic salvos against **SENTINEL-MK9**.
- Speed and combo streaks deal multiplier damage; fast typing (<0.85s/word) triggers **Critical Strikes**.
- Defeat the boss to unlock the prestigious **Titan Slayer** badge.

### 5. Post-Test Telemetry & Scorecard Export
- Interactive SVG timeline chart tracking second-by-second Net WPM, Raw WPM, and typo timestamps.
- **Finger Weakness Heatmap**: Pinpoints which specific characters caused the most misses.
- **Scorecard Generator**: Export a PNG card or copy summary text for Discord/Twitter/Reddit.

### 6. Progression & Tactical Badges
- Persistent local profile tracking XP, Level progression (`Cadet` to `Apex Grandmaster`), total tests, and average accuracy.
- Unlockable achievement medals: *First Blood*, *Deadly Precision (100% Acc)*, *Century Club (100+ WPM)*, *Apex Overdrive (130+ WPM)*, *Flow State*, and *Hardcore Survivor*.
